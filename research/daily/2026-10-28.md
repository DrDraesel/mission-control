"The design vocabulary for your coding agent": 1 skill + **24 commands** (`craft`, `polish`, `audit`, `critique`, `distill`, `animate`, `bolder`, `quieter`, `extract`, `document`, `init` …) + **62 deterministic detector rules** for AI-generated frontend tells, run by a self-contained binary — **no LLM, no API key**. Why it matters: this is the enforceable version of the taste rules we keep applying by hand (hallmark 38k★ does the same job with a 48-theme catalog; impeccable adds the **deterministic, CI-able detector**). +1,471★ in 24h, pushed today, created 2025-11-16 — and **invisible to all 30 topic queries**, caught only by the web lane.

## 4. Video-gen on 16 GB — what changed this week

- **ComfyUI v0.40.0 (10-13) template pack now ships `LTX-2.5 DFR: Image to Video`** — three-stage i2v with spatial + optional temporal upscaling and audio in the same pass. (This reverses the 10-06 note that the LTX-2.5 template was swapped out for Hunyuan.) Same release: Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo 8-step, Fish Audio Drama 3 voice-direction nodes, Higgsfield Genjutsu motion transfer, HeyGen Video 1.0 at 2K, and an **ARM64 NVIDIA Windows portable build**.
- **Wan on 8 GB: ComfyUI PR #17158** adds token-chunked attention/FFN for Wan → **720p × 81 frames runs with KSampler under 5 GB** (bitwise-identical output, ~+4% layer time). For a 16 GB card this makes Wan 2.2 5B 720p the practical everyday route instead of 480p.
- **LTX-2.5 official floor is 32 GB VRAM / 32 GB RAM**; measured offload routes: WanGP ≈5.9 GiB peak at 1280×704 T2V, ≈8.1 GiB at 960×960 i2v (RTX 4090 measurements, not 16 GB-verified). Wan 2.2 5B official floor is 24 GB with offload — the PR above is what actually makes 16 GB work.
- Blockers unchanged: **local ComfyUI is v0.25.0 (June build), 18 versions behind v0.43.0** — the LTX-2.5 DFR template, Wan chunking, Kandinsky6 and H3 nodes all need that update. This remains the single highest-value action in the project.
