### 2. Comfy-Org/ComfyUI_Nvidia_VFX_Nodes — 675★ — Apache-2.0 — **official RTX VFX nodes**
- Official Comfy-Org extension (formerly "Nvidia RTX Nodes for ComfyUI"), wrapping NVIDIA `nvidia-vfx` Python bindings:
  - **RTX Video Super Resolution** — upscale/denoise/deblur/enhance video frames
  - **RTX Video HDR** — SDR → BT.2020/PQ or HLG (true HDR output)
  - **RTX Video Frame Generation** — frame interpolation / slow-mo (**40-series+ only**)
- Requires CUDA-enabled PyTorch **cu130+** and an RTX GPU. Install via ComfyUI Manager.
- Pipeline value: upscale + HDR + interpolation post-steps with zero paid tools — **after** the pending ComfyUI update (needed anyway for Kandinsky6/H3/LTX2).
