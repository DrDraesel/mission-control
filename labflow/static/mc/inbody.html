Before you save

  • Check the patient name and the measurement date against the printout.
  • Leave fields blank if the printout does not show them — a blank is honest, a zero is not.
  • Enter body fat either in kg or % (or both) exactly as printed; the app does not recompute it.
  • If a number looks wrong later, add a corrected entry; the timeline keeps both.
